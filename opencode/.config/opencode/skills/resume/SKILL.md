## What to Add or Increase
- Concrete numbers: Use at least one number every three sentences to quantify your achievements.
- Leadership words: Incorporate 1–2 active words (like "managed" or "coordinated") every five sentences.
- Action verbs: Start every achievement sentence with a distinct action verb.
- Targeted buzzwords: Include 15–20 skills, acronyms, and keywords directly from the job posting.
- Densely packed text: Aim for a sweet spot of 475–600 words to maximize a single-page layout.
## What to Remove or Avoid
- Resume objectives: Delete your objective statement if you have more than 8 months of experience.
- Personal pronouns: Eliminate all uses of "I", "my", "me", "he", or "she".
- Collaborative fluff: Minimize passive "team player" phrases, as hiring managers penalize vague shared work.
- Graduation dates: Omit graduation years if you are over the age of 35 to reduce age bias. The average age for graduation is 22. Use that estimate to determine whether the graduation date should be removed.
- Excessive job history: Limit your detailed history to the 3–4 most recent positions.
- Short employment stints: Leave off jobs that lasted fewer than 9 months if they hint at a firing or quick exit.
